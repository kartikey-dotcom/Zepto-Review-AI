# Zepto Reviews AI — Customer Category Switching & Order Repetition Pattern Analysis

**Target Domain:** Quick Commerce (Q-Commerce) — Grocery, Instant Delivery & Expansion Categories  
**Target Organization:** Zepto (`com.zepto.customer`)  
**Core Strategic Objective:** Analyze customer review patterns to uncover why users repeat orders in primary core categories (daily groceries, milk, vegetables) and resist switching to higher-margin non-core categories (Electronics, Cosmetics, Zepto Cafe, Meat & Seafood, Baby Care).  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context

Zepto has achieved market leadership in 10-minute instant delivery for daily essentials (milk, bread, vegetables, quick snacks). However, **customer order repetition in core categories is high (81.4%)**, while **cross-category adoption in non-core categories remains low (18.6%)**.

When users open the Zepto app, they default to habitual, emergency grocery reorders rather than exploring new product vertical expansion categories.

**Zepto Reviews AI** analyzes 5,000 Play Store customer reviews to isolate and quantify the exact psychological, operational, and discovery barriers preventing cross-category switching.

---

## 2. Core Category Switching Friction Types

```
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+

|                                    |                       | 10-minute milk/grocery top-ups.            |
+------------------------------------+-----------------------+--------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 3. Product & Growth Strategic Recommendations

1. **Zepto Assured Quality Badge**: Display a "3-Day Easy Return & Verified Authentic" badge on Electronics & Cosmetics items.
2. **Contextual Cart Nudges**: Suggest relevant non-core add-ons during grocery checkout (e.g. "Add Phone Charger for Rs. 199").
3. **Elevated Category Navigation**: Redesign the app homepage search to elevate non-core category discovery tabs.
